Economic interpretation

The task approach asks which human responsibilities change with a routine-workload assumption. Personnel economics enters through skill readiness, training investment, attrition, mentor opportunity cost and the make-or-buy margin. Local stocks discipline context; they do not identify labour supply to this project.

IO is a fixed-coefficient gross activity model. Programme allocation is deterministic conditional optimisation. Neither identifies a causal AI or training effect. A causal extension needs treatment assignment, observed outcomes and a credible comparison design.

Three ledgers, three questions

  • Regional activity: Scottish production and work associated with the assumed spend.
  • Employer resources: transition and expected ongoing service costs.
  • Public programme: training capacity supported per pound. Guarantee exposure stays separate.

No social NPV or benefit-cost ratio is reported without benefit, additionality and opportunity-cost evidence.

Professional implementation

Python/SciPy and browser/HiGHS WebAssembly solve the same three-stage mixed-integer model. The local browser solver runs in a worker. A 15-second limit applies per optimisation stage; unfinished solves return errors instead of optimality claims. MILP has exponential worst-case complexity.
