# Two-minute demonstration script

**0:00–0:20 — Frame the question.** “This is an independent public-data assessment of a hypothetical AI data-centre investment in Scotland. The decision is how to prepare suppliers and workers, rather than infer permanent jobs from the capital headline.” Show the official-data and scenario badges.

**0:20–0:45 — Read the units.** “A £1bn constant-price capital scenario sends £400m to Scottish production. It supports around 3,981 FTE job-years over the build, or 1,327 annual average FTE across three years. Operation supports around 238 annual FTE, including 120 assumed on-site FTE.” Point to the schedule. “These quantities must not be added as contemporaneous jobs.”

**0:45–1:05 — Test a decision driver.** Change construction capture from 90% to 70%. “Local production capture materially changes Scottish activity.” Change duration from three to four years. “The same work spreads over more years.” Change the contract electricity price. “The bill changes; a price increase alone does not increase physical generation or jobs.”

**1:05–1:25 — Check the workforce evidence.** Open Workforce. “The occupations come from UK 2025 staffing shares transferred to Scottish industries. These are proxies. The apprenticeship counts below are real Scottish administrative data, but graduates are not necessarily available applicants.”

**1:25–1:45 — Show a feasible response.** Open Policy. “Conversion training and experienced recruitment can fit the base launch. A new electrical apprentice cohort takes an assumed 48 months, so the policy calculation flags its timing. The options overlap and are not added together.”

**1:45–2:00 — Make it reviewable.** Open Evidence. “The model reconciles with official published multipliers, includes a hand-solvable verification and exports all inputs. Download the current scenario and brief; the repository reproduces the case offline. A subsidy decision still needs a net welfare appraisal.” Reset controls.
