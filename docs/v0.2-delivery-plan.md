## Algorithm design before implementation

For each selected occupation-period target, define a required FTE, a readiness deadline and an engagement period. A candidate pathway has a role-specific eligibility flag, lead time, maximum cohort size, completion/match yield, attrition, transition costs and mentoring requirements. Programme capacities are scenario limits, never inferred from employment stocks or course existence.

Decision variables are integer cohort places plus continuous expected-FTE deficits. First minimise critical-role deficits. Fix that optimum within a small numerical tolerance; then minimise all deficits. Finally minimise discounted employer-plus-public resource cost conditional on the preceding coverage optima. Separate constraints cap each payer's transition budget, provider/pathway places and mentor hours. A pathway finishing after the deadline is ineligible. Return all solver statuses, constraint residuals and unfilled roles. Expected coverage is not a guarantee of realised qualified staff.

Build the same coefficient model into SciPy/HiGHS for the CLI and a locally bundled MIT-licensed HiGHS WebAssembly solver for the browser. Check both against a hand-solvable small allocation and across full-case inputs; do not substitute a greedy heuristic for an optimality claim. Document bounded MILP worst-case exponential complexity and solver time/optimality limits.
