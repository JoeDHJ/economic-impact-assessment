Haojian Dui · Independent portfolio case · 8-minute management briefing

| Slide | Message and visual | Decision / speaking note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The investment question | £1bn scenario; Scotland; three-year construction followed by operations | Ask what production and work stay in the region, and which workforce commitments can be delivered on time. |
| 2. Follow the spending | Capital component/capture waterfall: £1bn → £400m Scottish production | Explain producer capture, imported computers and basic-price accounting. Use `outputs/industries.csv` and the dashboard controls. |
| 3. Separate the phases | Dashboard annual FTE schedule | 3,981 FTE job-years over the build, 1,327 annual average FTE, then 238 annual operating FTE. State these units aloud. |
| 4. Explain value added | Construction output, GVA and compensation panel | £259.1m GVA is part of gross output; turnover is not a welfare benefit. Operations do not estimate centre revenue/GVA. |
| 5. Translate work into roles | Construction vs operation occupation tables | Electrical and construction trades matter during delivery; IT operations dominate assumed direct staffing. Staffing is a UK-to-Scotland proxy. |
| 6. Test delivery capacity | Actual SDS framework achievements, starts and in-training counts | Counts establish a training channel, not an available labor pool. ESS supports checking recruitment risk. |
| 7. Compare actions and timing | Three policy option cards | Conversion training and experienced recruitment fit the base opening. A new electrical cohort needs 48 assumed months; discuss later expansion. |
| 8. Set the next gate | Procurement, grid, workforce and net-appraisal checklist | Request a site-level roster and bill of quantities. Do not decide a subsidy from a gross activity multiplier. |
