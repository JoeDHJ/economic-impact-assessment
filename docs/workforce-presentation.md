| Slide | Message and exhibit | Spoken judgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Decision and scope | DV1 expansion / DV3 new-build; public financing, conditional planning inputs | “A financing announcement is not a capex bill or a verified staffing plan.” |
| 2. Evidence | Claim ledger and local-data definitions; 39 official observations | “Resident stocks, workplace jobs and applicants answer different questions.” |
| 3. Mechanism | Monthly construction chart and operating service roster | “Phasing changes occupational peaks; six continuous posts require 31.85 service FTE under these cover-hour assumptions.” |
| 4. Options | No programme, hire/contract, mixed and phased launch table | “Mixed capacity covers critical targets but leaves 17.83 role-period FTE overall. This is expected service capacity, not new jobs.” |
| 5. Binding decisions | Route utilisation plus constraint-sensitivity table | “Money alone does not remove the gap. A joint route/mentor expansion covers expected targets only if those qualified candidates and places exist.” |
| 6. Recommendation and gates | Verify roster and incumbents → confirm qualified pools and mentors → fund ongoing service costs → evaluate outcomes | “Do not approve staffing or public value on model output alone. Next evidence should replace the assumptions most likely to change the commitment.” |
